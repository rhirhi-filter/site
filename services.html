Choosing your manicure

Classic — quick maintenance: manual cuticle care, shaping, polish or gel. Natural and clean.

Smart (Russian / e-file) — our signature precision manicure: advanced e-file cuticle work, gel applied close to the cuticle, flawless and longer-lasting. Any e-file service is a Smart service, with or without colour.

Japanese — restorative care for natural nails using natural pastes and minerals. No gel, no colour — just healthy, glossy shine.

Hard Gel & Smart Hard Gel include removal (from another salon charged separately), manicure, gel colour and finish options — Cat Eye or Chrome on Hard Gel; builder base, French finish or repair (up to 2 nails), ombré or stamping on Smart. Nail extensions, more than 2 repairs, and nail art are charged separately.
