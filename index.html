Good to know

Questions, answered

Where are FILTER's salons in Abu Dhabi?
We have two branches: Al Raha (Al Faridah Complex) and Al Bateen, Marasy (Marasy, Building 4). Guests visit us from across Abu Dhabi — Yas Island, Khalifa City, Khalidiya and the Corniche.
Can I book the salon privately?
Yes — VIP clients can reserve an entire branch exclusively for bridal parties, celebrations or private appointments, subject to availability and VIP tier pricing. Enquire on WhatsApp.
What are your opening hours?
Both salons open daily 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM, with late evenings until 10:00 PM on Tuesdays and Fridays.
Which brands do you work with?
Kérastase, K18, Schwarzkopf, L'Oréal Professionnel, Authentic Beauty Concept, HD Brows and Nouveau Lashes.
Do you take a deposit?
Yes — AED 200 for hair and AED 50 for beauty. It acts as a non-refundable booking fee that secures your slot, and it comes off your final bill. We ask for 24 hours' notice to move an appointment.
Do you photograph your work?
We often take before-and-after photos for training and quality assurance. We never share them publicly, including on social media, without your consent — and you're always free to decline.
Is FILTER a ladies-only salon?
Yes — both branches are exclusively ladies-only, with complete privacy, strict hygiene standards and personalised care at every visit.
Are your staff professionally trained?
Yes. Every professional at FILTER is trained, certified and experienced — and our team regularly updates their skills to stay aligned with the latest techniques, trends and hygiene standards.
What are your opening hours?
Every day 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM, with late evenings until 10:00 PM on Tuesdays and Fridays — at both Al Raha and Al Bateen, Marasy.
